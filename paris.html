🥐 Paris, France

Paris invites you to slow down and notice the details: sunlight on old stone, the scent of a neighborhood bakery, and the pleasure of getting a little lost with friends.

🗼 Landmarks and long walks

The Eiffel Tower is unforgettable, but the pleasure of Paris is just as much in the walk there. Follow the Seine, cross a bridge, and let the view change with every turn. Around the Louvre and through the quieter streets of the Marais, history feels close enough to touch.

🥐 Culinary delights

Some of the best Paris plans begin at a bakery counter. Share a flaky croissant over coffee, pick up a picnic for the riverbank, or linger over a simple meal at a neighborhood bistro. The ritual of taking time to eat is part of the city's charm.

Culture at every pace

Spend an afternoon in a museum, browse a bookstall along the river, or find a small gallery tucked between cafés. Paris rewards curiosity, whether you have a full itinerary or only time to follow the next interesting street.

A moment to remember

What makes Paris meaningful is how easily an ordinary afternoon becomes a memory: friends trading bites of dessert, pausing to admire a view, and turning the walk back into the long way home. The city makes room for conversation as much as for sightseeing.
